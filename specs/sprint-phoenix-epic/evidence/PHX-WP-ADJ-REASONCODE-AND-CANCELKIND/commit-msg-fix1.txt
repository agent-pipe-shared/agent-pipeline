fix(pipeline-core): guard reasonCode against RegExp#test's array coercion

RegExp.prototype.test coerces its argument with String(...) before
matching, so a bare `CODE.test(value.reasonCode)` admitted a
single-element array whose sole entry matched the pattern
(String(["SOME_CODE"]) === "SOME_CODE") even though the published
schema types reasonCode as a plain string. All three reasonCode call
sites in agent-decision-journal.mjs (validateAgentDecisionEvent,
validateCommandOfferEvent, validateLegacyImportObservationEvent) now
carry the same `typeof value.reasonCode !== "string"` guard the
pre-existing revalidationTrigger check already used. Adds one
parametrized regression test covering all three validators.
