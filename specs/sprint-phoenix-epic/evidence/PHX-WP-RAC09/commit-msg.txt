feat(pipeline-core): render a duplicated offer-lifecycle record invalid on replay

R-AC-09's "duplicated" trigger was credited to the store's idempotencyKey
mechanism, but that answers a different question: it makes an identical retry
safe on the STORE's identity (no second write, divergent retry refused, two
same-key records make every projection throw). The offer lifecycle correlates
on eventId, which no stream scan indexes and which the envelope keeps as a
token independent of idempotencyKey, so two records carrying one lifecycle
eventId under different idempotency keys both land and both replay as a valid
stream -- leaving a dependent outcome's offerEventId resolving to more than one
record, which is the ambiguity this criterion forbids.

projectCommandOfferReplay renders such a journal integrity "invalid" with a
typed reasonCode, and an optional caller-supplied `journaled` refuses the
duplicate before append at the single shared append seam. Additive: omitting
`journaled` leaves every existing path byte-for-byte unchanged, so the
pre-existing R-AC-13 delegation test still passes unmodified.
