# Elephant decisions under D26, 2026-10-19 (0.7.0 Alfred candidate)

decisions to the Elephant. Each must be decided with a recorded rationale and presented to the
Critic batch. E9 and E10 are two more scope rulings the Elephant needed to brief the D20 agent
work. These are Elephant decisions, not PO decisions; the PO can reverse any of them at
acceptance. Every entry is marked **to Critic batch**. E3 and E7 are also flagged for the
Critic specifically, because they touch guardrail and routing text.
