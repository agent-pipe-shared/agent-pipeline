# PO design input — gate-1 rework directive (captured 2026-08-28)

Structured extraction of the material PO input given at the first PRD gate.
Sanitized; no transcript. Source evidence for the rework wave; the gap
analysis (`gap-analysis-2026-08-28.md`) and the architecture doctrine
(`agent-first-architecture.md`) execute it.

## Verdict

The PRD presented at the gate was **rejected as far too shallow for a sprint
of this size**. The PO's assessment, distilled:

- The design under-serves Alfred's **primary theme: agentic architecture**.
  Nearly all elementary requirements from the architecture issues are
  missing from the PRD's own text.
- The governance topics (control integrity, mechanical governance,
  measurable rigor) are good and important — **but they are bycatch
  ("Beifang")**. They must be presented as the supporting substructure of the
  architecture mandate, not as the sprint's headline.
- Depth, not breadth, is the failure: for an epic this large, a design that
  names its subjects without working them out is insufficient.

## The four questions the design must answer at depth

Stated by the PO as the test of the rework:

1. **Which best practices** define good agentic architecture, concretely —
   and what are the right approaches?
2. **How do we ensure them later in the enforcement layer** — not as prompt
   guidance but mechanically?
3. **How is documentation done for the machine**, so agents can actually
   find things again instead of thinking only within single sessions?
4. **How is the architecture represented user-facing?**

## Directives

1. Re-read the architecture issues (#99, #104, #106, #109) in full and audit
   the design against their elementary requirements; close the gaps in the
   design's own text — absorbing content, not referencing it.
2. Track D becomes the design's center of gravity; Tracks A/B/C are argued
   as what makes D's claims true (measured enforcement, honest telemetry),
   never as the lead story.
3. The standing constraints hold unchanged — including ≥1 independent Critic
   round per design document before the PO reviews it.
