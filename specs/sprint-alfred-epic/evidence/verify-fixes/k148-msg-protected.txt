fix(pipeline-core): publish address slots complete and tolerate a concurrent slot in the namespace check

Two processes on one home preparing different repositories made the fixed address namespace listing differ between its two reads, and the first process failed with SP-ADDRESS fixed address namespace changed. A slot another process was still creating (mkdir, then anchor.json) was observable half-made and failed as a missing or foreign slot.

The namespace check now accepts a changed listing only when every originally read name is still present and every new name validates as a complete slot, with at most two accepted changes before the existing refusal. A slot is staged beside the namespace with its anchor.json and renamed into place, so it is never observable without its anchor. Foreign names, over-bound namespaces, removed slots and tampered anchors are refused as before.
