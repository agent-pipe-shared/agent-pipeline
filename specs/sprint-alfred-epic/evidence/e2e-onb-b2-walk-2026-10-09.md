- Hypothesis H1, not measured: `project-onboarding-v3.mjs:809-816` speaks of a "committed global `human_approval: chat`", and the walk had no commit (item 4 refused by the host), so the policy may be honoured only once committed. - Consequence: under `chat` the walk cannot reach `submit-plan` by following returned actions, and whether the chat lane is reachable at all once the policy is committed is the first thing the next walk must measure.
