# CYB-4 canonical threat-model reference

This package is the repository's candidate-bound reference instance for the
CYB-4 threat-model contract. It records the agent-pipeline delivery boundary:
untrusted model proposals cannot turn lifecycle text into risk approval.

The immutable model and its separate human/policy receipt are evidence, not
the source of product authority. They bind the observed candidate and policy
revision named in their closed records.
