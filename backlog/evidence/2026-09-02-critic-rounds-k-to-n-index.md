# Critic rounds K through N — index and recovery instructions

The 2026-09-01/02 release block ran four independent review rounds. Two of
them reported that the evidence they were asked to judge against lived only in
the gitignored `scratch/` directory, and one of them demonstrated the
consequence: a green test artifact was overwritten in place by a later red
re-run, and a claim in the round's own record could no longer be substantiated
from anything in the evidence set.

This file and the artifacts beside it are the correction. They are tracked, so
the measuring stick for these rounds survives the next `scratch/` clean.

## Artifacts here

- `2026-09-02-critic-round-k-findings.md`, `2026-09-02-critic-round-l-findings.md` —
  the neutral findings registries. These are the input contract for a

  each round was dispatched with: commands, exit codes, counts, artifact paths
  and disclosed deviations.

## Recovering the specs

The specs handed to these rounds were stripped copies of backlog items, not the
items themselves — an item's own Triage and Closure prose records a prior
verdict about the very thing a Critic is independently judging, so it is
removed before dispatch. The stripped copies were written to `scratch/` and are
not tracked, but they are **regenerable and deterministic**:

```
node plugins/pipeline-core/scripts/backlog-item-strip-for-dispatch.mjs \
  --item <the tracked item path> --out <destination>
```

The items themselves are tracked:

- `backlog/items/2026-09-01-an-authorized-rebase-demands-a-fresh-po-signature-after-every-conflict.md`
  — the rebase work package, rounds K (Requirement 3) and M (Requirements 1, 2, 4, 5).
- `backlog/items/2026-09-01-push-init-cannot-satisfy-its-own-doc-reconciliation-check.md`
  — round K.
- `backlog/items/2026-09-01-three-onboarding-suites-pass-locally-and-fail-in-ci.md`
  — rounds L and N.

## What is deliberately NOT here

The per-suite test output the claims records cite (`scratch/evidence-rebwire/`,
`scratch/nva-roundl/evidence/`, `scratch/logs/`, `scratch/f4-*.txt`) is
machine-regenerated by re-running the named command and stays untracked, per
ADR-0063's directory-kinds table. What that table asks to be tracked is the
gate-cited record — the claim, its command, its exit code and its deviations —
and that is what sits here.

The Critic reports themselves are not tracked either. Each round's reviewing
dispatch wrote its own notes under `scratch/dispatch/<codename>-<hex>/`, and
those are gone with the next clean. The findings registries above are the
durable distillation, which is what a later reader actually needs.
